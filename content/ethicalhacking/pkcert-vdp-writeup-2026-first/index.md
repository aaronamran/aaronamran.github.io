<p class="lead mb-4">Due to the rules of the Vulnerability Disclosure Program (VDP), this writeup will not disclose specific details regarding the vulnerability I discovered on one of the Government of Pakistan's public-facing digital assets. You can find their <a href="https://vdp.pkcert.gov.pk/public-dashboard" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">official VDP here</a>.</p>

![Government of Pakistan Cert](/images/VDP-Certificate-PKC-CPVRO-26-0111-aaronamran.png)

![Acknowledged Contributors](/images/pkgov-acknowledged-contributors.png)

<p class="lead mb-4">I identified the security flaw and reported it to PKCERT on 8 May 2026.</p>

![First Email](/images/vuln-submission-acknowledgement.png)

<p class="lead mb-4">After a few months of waiting, they sent me an update email on 7 September 2026, stating that the report is still under remediation.</p>

![Second Email](/images/progress-update.png)
 
<p class="lead mb-4">Finally, I received a formal acknowledgement via email on 7 October 2026 that includes the PDF certificate and discovered that my name is also listed in their VDP Hall of Fame.</p>

![Third Email](/images/email-appreciation.png)
